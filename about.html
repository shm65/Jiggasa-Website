Our Story

Why Jiggasa?

Every student is curious. But sometimes a topic is difficult, an explanation is not enough, or a student simply needs more time and practice.

Jiggasa was created to make learning available beyond the traditional classroom — allowing students to watch, practise, revise and learn at their own pace.

"The right question can open the door to understanding." That idea is at the heart of Jiggasa.

Founder & Educator

Sarawar Husen

Creator of Jiggasa (জিজ্ঞাসা)

Jiggasa was created by Sarawar Husen with the belief that education should encourage students to understand, question and think independently.

Rather than focusing only on completing a syllabus, Jiggasa aims to create a learning environment where students can explore concepts, practise regularly and gradually become confident learners.
